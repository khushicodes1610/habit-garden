# Habit Garden

## Tagline

**Plant habits. Harvest success.**

---

## Vision

Habit Garden transforms habit tracking into an engaging and rewarding experience. Instead of simply checking off tasks, users nurture a virtual garden that grows with every completed habit. The application encourages consistency through gamification, beautiful visuals, and meaningful progress, making self-improvement feel enjoyable rather than repetitive.

---

## Problem Statement

Most habit trackers rely on checklists and streak counters, which can become repetitive over time. Habit Garden makes habit building more enjoyable by rewarding consistency with a living virtual garden that grows alongside the user.

---

## Objectives

- Help users build positive habits.
- Encourage daily consistency.
- Visualize progress through a growing garden.
- Make self-improvement fun and engaging.
- Provide insightful statistics and streak tracking.

---

## Target Users

- Students
- Professionals
- Anyone trying to build better habits
